Teaching Philosophy

I build my teaching philosophy on combining theoretical knowledge with practical applications. I use case studies and interactive learning methods to ensure students understand real situations they will encounter in a clinical setting.

I take care to integrate current audiological practices into the curriculum by following technological developments. I adopt a project-based learning approach to help students develop their research skills.
